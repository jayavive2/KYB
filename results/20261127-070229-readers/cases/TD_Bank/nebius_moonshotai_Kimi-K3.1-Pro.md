### Sanctions and export controls

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2021-12-23]** OFAC announced a $115,005.04 settlement with TD Bank, N.A. for apparent violations of the North Korea Sanctions Regulations and the Foreign Narcotics Kingpin Sanctions Regulations. TD Bank processed 1,479 transactions totaling $382,685.38 and maintained nine accounts for employees of North Korea’s UN mission without an OFAC license, and separately maintained accounts for an SDN-listed U.S. resident and allowed $35,514.13 in transactions by that person; the apparent violations were voluntarily self-disclosed and non-egregious. ### Data breaches and cyber incidents

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2023-02-01]** A breach at TD Bank partner NCB Management Services began on February 1, 2023 and was detected on February 4, affecting more than 1 million individuals, primarily TD Bank customers. Names, Social Security numbers, account and card details were exposed and listed on the dark web, prompting class actions and regulatory scrutiny. [S49](https://securityblueprints.io/data-breaches/ncb-management-services-data-breach-february-2023)
- **[MEDIUM · confirmed · 2025-02]** TD Bank disclosed in February 2025 that a former employee had unauthorized access to customer information between August and December 2022, affecting at least eight Massachusetts residents; the employee was terminated and TD worked with law enforcement. Coverage of the confirmed breach said names, contact details, account numbers and, in some cases, Social Security numbers were exposed, TD offered identity protection, and a class action alleging negligence and inadequate data security remained active, with litigation reported as progressing in 2025. [S50](https://www.dexpose.io/bank-data-breaches), [S52](https://www.teiss.co.uk/news/td-bank-discloses-new-data-breach-affecting-massachusetts-customers-18209)
- **[MEDIUM · confirmed · 2026-06-15]** TD reported to Massachusetts regulators on June 15, 2026 that an employee had unauthorized access to U.S. customer records from January 7 to January 30, 2026. The insider incident potentially exposed names, addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, Social Security numbers, account numbers and transactional data; no external hacking or further misuse was reported at disclosure. [S48](https://www.upguard.com/news/td-data-breach-2026-06-17), [S51](https://dailyhodl.com/2026/07/10/td-bank-data-breach-exposes-customer-social-security-numbers-and-account-details-in-insider-incident)
- **[MEDIUM · confirmed · 2026-09-15]** TD Bank disclosed to Massachusetts regulators on September 15, 2026 a data security event that may have exposed customer personal and financial information; 54 Massachusetts residents were identified, while the nationwide total was not disclosed. Potentially exposed data included Social Security numbers, government-issued identification, pay stubs, W-2 forms, financial account information and payment-card numbers, and TD offered two years of Fraud-Defender identity monitoring. [S52](https://www.teiss.co.uk/news/td-bank-discloses-new-data-breach-affecting-massachusetts-customers-18209), [S53](https://www.claimdepot.com/data-breach/td-bank-2026)

TD Bank twice denied the couple's Regulation E refund request, stating once that there was no error and once that the transfers were authorized. [S47](https://abc7ny.com/post/online-banking-zelle-app-protect-from-ransomware-bank/11748085)

### Leadership and governance

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2024-10]** A Senate letter on TD Bank’s DOJ resolution, which included a historic $3 billion fine, said bank leadership knowingly presided over a criminally deficient anti-money-laundering program and high-level executives knew of serious problems, yet DOJ did not charge them and structured the charges to shift legal liability to holding company TD BHC. Commentary on the plea agreement and consent order said senior management repeatedly ignored warnings and speculated, without confirmation, that one or more whistleblowers may have helped build the government’s case. CFPB Director Rohit Chopra criticized TD Bank management for prioritizing growth and mergers over fixing the problems.
