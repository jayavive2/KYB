### Financial crime

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2024-10-10]** On October 10, 2024, TD Bank N.A. (TDBNA) and its parent TD Bank US Holding Company pleaded guilty to Bank Secrecy Act violations and money laundering conspiracy, agreeing to pay $1.8 billion in DOJ penalties — the largest ever imposed under the BSA and the first time a national bank pleaded guilty to conspiring to launder money — as part of a coordinated resolution with FinCEN (a record $1.3 billion penalty), the OCC, and the Federal Reserve, with total penalties of almost $3.1 billion. The bank admitted AML program failures spanning January 2014 to October 2023 that left roughly 92% of transaction volume (about $18.3 trillion) unmonitored and enabled three money laundering networks, one assisted by five TD Bank employees, to move more than $670 million through TD Bank accounts between 2019 and 2023.
