### Financial crime

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2024-10-10]** TD Bank, N.A. and its parent company TD Bank US Holding Company pleaded guilty in October 2024 to conspiring to fail to maintain an anti-money laundering program, fail to file accurate Currency Transaction Reports, and launder monetary instruments. TD Bank agreed to pay $1.8 billion in penalties to the DOJ—the largest BSA penalty ever—and the total coordinated resolution with FinCEN and OCC reached almost $3.1 billion. The bank failed to maintain an effective AML program from January 2014 to October 2023, with 92% of transaction volume going unmonitored (~$18.3 trillion), enabling three money laundering networks to transfer more than $670 million through TD Bank accounts. ### Sanctions and export controls

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2024-10-10]** FinCEN assessed a record $1.3 billion civil penalty against TD Bank for chronic AML failures, including failure to detect and report suspicious activity involving high-risk jurisdictions and funnel account activity. ### Data breaches and cyber incidents

### Leadership and governance

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2024-10]** TD Bank's leadership knowingly presided over a criminally deficient anti-money laundering program between January 2014 and October 2023, allowing the bank to launder money for fentanyl and human trafficking rings. High-level executives, including the person who became the bank's chief AML officer, knew of serious problems but failed to correct them. DOJ charged only two relatively low-level employees while structuring penalties through the holding company (TD BHC) to avoid full liability on the bank and its executives. [S56](https://www.warren.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/media/doc/warren_letter_to_doj_re_td_bank.pdf), [S55](https://www.corporatecomplianceinsights.com/how-earn-fine-td-bank-masterclass-compliance-failures) _(2 reports merged.)_
- **[MEDIUM · confirmed · 2024-09]** CFPB Director Rohit Chopra stated that 'TD Bank's management clearly cared more about growth and expanding its empire through mergers' than treating customers fairly, criticizing leadership priorities in connection with the bank's illegal furnishing of inaccurate and fraudulent information to consumer reporting companies affecting hundreds of thousands of customers. [S7](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/newsroom/cfpb-orders-td-bank-to-pay-28-million-for-breakdowns-that-illegally-tarnished-consumer-credit-reports)
