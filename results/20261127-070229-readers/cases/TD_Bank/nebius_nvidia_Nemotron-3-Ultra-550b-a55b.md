### Financial crime

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2024-10-10]** TD Bank, N.A. and its parent TD Bank US Holding Company pleaded guilty to conspiring to violate the Bank Secrecy Act, fail to file accurate Currency Transaction Reports, and launder monetary instruments. ### Data breaches and cyber incidents

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2023-02]** In February 2023, NCB Management Services, a vendor for TD Bank, suffered a data breach exposing over one million records of TD Bank customers, including names, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, and bank account information. The breach led to class action lawsuits and regulatory scrutiny, and TD Bank filed a breach notice with the Maine Attorney General. [S49](https://securityblueprints.io/data-breaches/ncb-management-services-data-breach-february-2023)
- **[MEDIUM · confirmed · 2026-01]** In January 2026, a TD Bank employee accessed customer records without authorization between January 7 and January 30, compromising names, addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, Social Security numbers, account numbers, and transactional data. The breach was confirmed in June 2026, reported to regulators, and TD Bank notified affected customers and offered additional safeguards. [S48](https://www.upguard.com/news/td-data-breach-2026-06-17), [S51](https://dailyhodl.com/2026/07/10/td-bank-data-breach-exposes-customer-social-security-numbers-and-account-details-in-insider-incident)
- **[MEDIUM · confirmed · 2026-09]** In September 2026, TD Bank disclosed a data security event to the Massachusetts Office of Consumer Affairs and Business Regulation, affecting at least 54 Massachusetts residents. The potentially exposed information included names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, addresses, government-issued identification, pay stubs, tax forms, financial account information, and credit or debit card numbers. TD Bank offered affected customers a two-year identity monitoring service. [S52](https://www.teiss.co.uk/news/td-bank-discloses-new-data-breach-affecting-massachusetts-customers-18209), [S53](https://www.claimdepot.com/data-breach/td-bank-2026)
- **[MEDIUM · confirmed · 2022-08]** Between August and December 2022, a former TD Bank employee gained unauthorized access to customer information, affecting at least eight Massachusetts residents. The breach was reported to Massachusetts regulators in February 2025; TD Bank terminated the employee and worked with law enforcement to investigate. [S52](https://www.teiss.co.uk/news/td-bank-discloses-new-data-breach-affecting-massachusetts-customers-18209)
- **[LOW · alleged · 2022-04]** In April 2022, a couple reported that their TD Bank accounts were targeted by thieves using the Zelle app after their home computer was infected with ransomware. TD Bank denied their refund requests under Regulation E, stating the transfers were authorized. [S47](https://abc7ny.com/post/online-banking-zelle-app-protect-from-ransomware-bank/11748085)
