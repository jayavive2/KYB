### Financial crime

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2026-01]** Wilfredo Aquino, a former TD Bank assistant store manager, pleaded guilty in January 2026 to conspiring to launder monetary instruments and in October 2026 to accepting bribes as a bank employee. Between 2019 and February 2021, Aquino facilitated the movement of approximately $474 million through TD Bank accounts by accepting bribes, including over $11,000 in retail gift cards. From April 2022 to November 2023, he also facilitated the laundering of $4.8 million to Colombia by opening fraudulent accounts, issuing debit cards, and unblocking restricted cards. He received over $6,000 in bribes in cash and via digital payment networks. ### Leadership and governance

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2024-09]** TD Bank was ordered by the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) to pay $28 million in 2024 for repeatedly providing inaccurate and fraudulent information to consumer reporting agencies, including data on bankruptcies and delinquencies, and failing to properly investigate disputes for over a year. The CFPB found systemic failures in TD Bank's management and governance, with leadership prioritizing growth over compliance. [S8](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/newsroom/cfpb-orders-td-bank-to-pay-28-million-for-breakdowns-that-illegally-tarnished-consumer-credit-reports), [S4](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/enforcement/actions/td-bank-na-furnishing-2024) _Also: Regulatory and enforcement actions._

This executive accountability action was tied to governance failures and the impact on the U.S. retail business. [S58](https://stories.td.com/ca/en/news/2025-01-17-td-bank-group-accelerates-ceo-transition-3b-announces-board-an)
