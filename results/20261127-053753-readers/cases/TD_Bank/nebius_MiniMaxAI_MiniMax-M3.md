### Financial crime

### Sanctions and export controls

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2024-10-10]** FinCEN assessed a record $1.3 billion civil penalty against TD Bank for willful failures to maintain an adequate AML program and file SARs on thousands of suspicious transactions totaling approximately $1.5 billion. The Consent Order found that TD Bank's monitoring gaps resulted in approximately $18.3 trillion in unmonitored transaction activity, and that the bank failed to address funnel account activity involving high-risk countries. The order also noted that a former TD Bank branch manager was suspected of colluding to circumvent reporting requirements, and that the bank's investigation failed to escalate potential Cuba sanctions issues. [S1](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/enforcement_action/2024-10-10/FinCEN-TD-Bank-Consent-Order-508FINAL.pdf), [S9](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-assesses-record-13-billion-penalty-against-td-bank), [S39](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy2640) _Also: Regulatory and enforcement actions._
- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2021-12-23]** OFAC announced a $115,005.04 settlement with TD Bank, N.A. for apparent violations of the North Korea Sanctions Regulations and the Foreign Narcotics Kingpin Sanctions Regulations. In the first matter, TDBNA processed 1,479 transactions totaling $382,685.38 and maintained nine accounts for employees of the North Korean mission to the UN without a license. In the second matter, TDBNA maintained accounts for a U.S. resident on OFAC's SDN List, resulting in 145 apparent violations. The apparent violations were voluntarily self-disclosed and deemed non-egregious. [S10](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/917121/download?inline=), [S11](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20211223_33) _Also: Regulatory and enforcement actions._
