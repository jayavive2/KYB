### Financial crime

- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2024-10-10]** TD Bank N.A. and its parent TD Bank US Holding Company pleaded guilty to conspiring to fail to maintain an AML program, fail to file accurate Currency Transaction Reports, and launder money, agreeing to pay approximately $1.8 billion in penalties (reported as up to $3 billion) and to remediate their AML compliance program. [S13](https://www.justice.gov/criminal/case/united-states-america-v-td-bank-na), [S14](https://www.justice.gov/criminal/case/united-states-america-v-td-bank-us-holding-company), [S36](https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/td-bank-penalties-1.7348819) _Also: Regulatory and enforcement actions._
- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2026-01]** Former TD Bank assistant store manager Wilfredo Aquino pleaded guilty to conspiring to launder monetary instruments, having facilitated the movement of approximately $474 million through TD Bank accounts for a money‑laundering network. [S30](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/td-bank-insider-pleads-guilty-facilitating-money-laundering)
- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2026-01-21]** TD Bank employee Nunez pleaded guilty to facilitating a Colombian ATM money‑laundering scheme, moving over $26 million from the United States to Colombia via hundreds of debit cards and ATM withdrawals. [S32](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/td-bank-insider-pleads-guilty-facilitating-colombian-atm-money-laundering-scheme)
- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2026-01-30]** Former TD Bank employee Leonardo Ayala pleaded guilty to conspiring to launder monetary instruments and receiving bribes, having laundered approximately $5.5 million to Colombia. [S33](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/former-td-bank-employee-pleads-guilty-accepting-bribes-laundering-55-million-colombia)
- **[HIGH · confirmed · 2026-10-06]** Former TD Bank employee Wilfredo Aquino pleaded guilty to conspiring to launder monetary instruments and receiving bribes, having laundered approximately $4.8 million to Colombia and received over $8,000 in bribes. [S31](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/former-td-bank-employee-pleads-guilty-accepting-bribes-laundering-48-million-colombia)
- **[MEDIUM · confirmed · 2026-07-16]** Wilfredo Aquino was sentenced to prison for facilitating money laundering, having previously pleaded guilty to conspiring to launder monetary instruments. [S34](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/two-td-bank-insiders-sentenced-prison-facilitating-money-laundering-fraud)
- **[MEDIUM · confirmed · 2026-06-10]** Leonardo Ayala was sentenced to two years in prison and three years of supervised release for accepting bribes and laundering more than $5.5 million to Colombia. [S35](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/td-bank-insider-sentenced-prison-accepting-bribes-laundering-millions-colombia)
